# Hypothesis card — Insider Cluster Buys

**card_id:** 2026-08-28-insider-clusters
**mechanism family:** informed-counterparty / forced-information (NEW family
— shot #1). Section 16 insiders must disclose; cluster purchases (>=3
distinct insiders, >=$200k, 30-day filing window) are the rare public
signal generated by people with genuine information buying with their own
money. Documented in literature and among the few effects that persisted
post-publication.

## Local scouting evidence (2026-08-28)

- Signal built from the complete SEC DERA Form 3/4/5 archive, 2006–2026
  (3.47M transactions; 13,562 cluster-buy events; filing-date timed).
- On the covered large-cap panel (562 events, 4% coverage — the WEAKEST
  venue for this effect): +3.84%/63d excess (t=4.0), +5.48%/126d (t=4.2).
- Same-symbol random-date control: timing-specific gap +2.14%/63d (t=2.05),
  +3.54%/126d (t=2.42) — BELOW the local t>=2.5 bar, but on <5% of events.
- Cluster sells: near-uninformative (as literature predicts).

## QC specification (the decisive test)

Custom-data bridge: upload `cluster_buys.csv` (13,562 events) to QC Object
Store. Algorithm subscribes to each event's symbol as it fires (QC maps
delisted tickers -> kills both coverage skew and survivorship at once),
buys at next open, equal weight ~2%, max 50 concurrent, exits after 126
trading days. Period 2007-01-01..2022-12-31 (2023+ = family holdout).
