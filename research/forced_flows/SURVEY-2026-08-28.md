## Reverse-split discipline rules (from the survey, non-negotiable)

  bounds (TTSH continuing stub fell −48% in 2 weeks).
- Parse street-name treatment per deal: some (Cyanotech-style) pay
  RECORD holders only → broker-held shares need a DRS transfer or skip.
- Prefer post-vote entry (near-riskless residual 1-3%) over
  post-announcement (5-10% spread but abandonment risk).
- Sector screen each issuer (cannabis/banks common in this universe).
- Scanner noise: most 14A reverse-split hits are routine compliance
  splits (fractional-cash only) — SC 13E3 form is the high-precision
  signal; 14A hits require reading.
